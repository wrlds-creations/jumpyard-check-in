The remaining sequence is intentionally one ticket at a time: contract/config, then synth and deploy preflight, then explicitly approved AWS/Live steps, then controlled quote, draft, payment, redeem, and visitor tests.

Guardrails:

- Dev/Playground must stay available and untouched unless a later ticket explicitly scopes a dev change.

- AWS resource creation, Roller Live reads, Roller Live writes, webhook registration, payment, and redeem each require their own scoped ticket and explicit approval where listed.
- Refund/cancel after the first internal Live payment remains manual outside the app unless a later ticket explicitly scopes it.
